# The store of the search of duplicates of a content type: `duplicates`

`types/<type>/duplicates.duckdb` — a DuckDB file (owner, 2026-11-22: "let us move to DuckDB"), made by its one script
`app/src/main/resources/duplicate/store.sql` and made anew, empty, as its tables are other than the script makes
(`DuckDatabase`): everything in it is made again by the next search, the user's words on the pairs kept elsewhere. The
candidates are written whole by every search, appended in the order of their key, no index of them kept.

| Table | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `duplicate_candidate` | two works of different sites that may be one, as the last search of the duplicates found them — of every work the likeliest of every other site — with how alike each layer of theirs is: the title, the description, the size, every facet the sites share (`facets`, "<facet>=<cosine>" a line), whether the dictionary of authors gives them one author, fully or in part (`same_author`), and the works it was found for (`found_for`: 1 the first, 2 the second, 3 both); the title compared letter by letter — the cosine of the trigrams (`title`) and how much of either the other has (`title_within`, `title_holding`) — and the names of the authors the same (`authors_alike`, `authors_within`, `authors_holding`, none of a pair of no names); the other way, the title by meaning (`title_vector`) and the other texts letter by letter — the description, the sites' names of the fandoms, the sites' lines of the characters (`description_letters`, `fandom_letters`, `character_letters`), none of a pair either of whose has none — and an author only in part one (`partly_same_author`); the words of either work the search compared them by, stated or worked out of the characters (`words`, `other_words`): what the pairs are sorted by their size by; and, worked out when the search fills the table — what a page of them is chosen and ordered by in the store, a pass of the columns of its filter and its sort, no index kept —: the chance of one work and of one series by the models of the answers (`same_chance`, `series_chance`), the user's word on the pair as the search or the last answer left it (`answer`, none of an open one; `answer_derived`: of one series by the series), whether the two works have every normalized author and the title alike (`same_authors`, `same_title`), and the key of every other sort (`by_size`, `by_series_size`: the mean size times either chance; `by_doubt`, `by_series_doubt`: how near either chance is to where the answers change; `by_formula`, `by_interest`, `by_grade`: the found work's worth by the prediction and the user's marks of interest times the chance) |
| `duplicate_value` | how rare every value two works are compared by is among every work of the type, as the last search of the duplicates weighed it (`rarity`, ln(works / works having it)), by the value's name (`name`, "characters:wikidata:Q…"): what a pair the search did not keep is compared by as it is shown; made anew whole by every search |
| `duplicate_search` | when the duplicates were last looked for, by which encoder, of how many works, and the models of the user's answers as the plugin of pairs (`model`, its id) packed them: of one work (`same_model`) and of one series (`series_model`), none of a kind no pair was said to be of; when they were last fitted (`fitted_at`) and how long it took (`fit_millis`) |
